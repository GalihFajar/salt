Welcome. 

This blog is called *Salt*. The name comes from the old saying — take it with a grain of salt. Everything here is a draft of my thinking, not a final verdict. Opinions change, understanding deepens, and what I write today I might disagree with tomorrow.

I'm writing mostly for myself — to clarify my thoughts, to keep a record, and to practice the craft of putting ideas into words. If someone else finds it useful, that's a bonus.

No comments. No analytics. No newsletter. Just words and an [RSS feed](/salt/rss.xml) if you want to follow along.
